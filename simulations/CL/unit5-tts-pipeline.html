How does written text become spoken sound?

Speech synthesis (TTS) accepts text input and generates speech output. This page follows the booklet: linguistic hierarchy, then the TTS process from text analysis to waveform generation.

Learning summary

Text-to-speech (TTS) synthesis accepts text input and generates synthesized speech output. The goal is to enable machines to “speak” clearly, naturally and may even be expressively.

HIERARCHYSentence → grammar → words/phrases → phones → acoustics.
NORMALISEChange written text into “speakable” words.
PHONEMEMap words to phonetic labels.
PROSODYPredict intonation, duration, and energy.
WAVEFORMTransform the linguistic representation into sound.
EVALUATEObjective: math distance (e.g. Euclidean). Subjective: humans listen & score (MOS).
