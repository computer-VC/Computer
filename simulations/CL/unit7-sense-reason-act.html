Sense · Reason · Act

How do robots “think” through a task?

Like humans, robots repeatedly sense, reason, and act. This page also introduces three human-like reasoning levels: skill-based, rule-based, and knowledge-based.

SENSING REASONING ACTION 3 LEVELS
01Robot

Learning summary

A robot performing a task involves sensing, reasoning and action. Human-like reasoning can be programmed into the micro-processor.

SKILLBasic automated tasks; intuitive sensory-motor behaviour.
RULEMore involved automated processes using instructions and patterns.
KNOWLEDGEAI robots that analyse situations and adapt — needs more data & ML.

Generally, the more complex and dynamic the task, the more knowledge-based reasoning is needed.
