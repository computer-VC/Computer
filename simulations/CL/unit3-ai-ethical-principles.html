AI Ethical Principles

What should AI be allowed to do?

AI can mark essays, track contacts, drive cars, or invent fake videos. Before we cheer for speed, we ask: is this good or bad, right or wrong — and for whom?

This lesson explains ethics, then the five common AI ethical principles, with short concept stories for Tasks 1–3.

01Ethics

Learning summary

AI ethics is a system of principles. These principles guide us to consider both the positive and negative impacts of AI when developing related technologies and using them in real applications.

Ethical considerations are often very complex; thus, it is important to promote more thoughtful attention to the complex ethical problems. Controversies may arise about the future application of “Advanced AI”.

TRANSPARENCYAI processing should be interpretable and explainable to users.
JUSTICE & FAIRNESSEnsure equality, inclusion, and diversity; avoid bias and discrimination.
BENEFICENCEDo no harm; prioritise safety and well-being.
RESPONSIBILITYUphold accountability and honesty for AI’s impact.
PRIVACYProtect and secure sensitive data under relevant rules.
